About Me

Hi, I'm Nati. I'm a high school student living in New Zealand with a strong interest in astrophysics, exoplanets, and aerospace.

This blog serves as my digital notebook where I sit down with my textbook, calculator, and pencil to work out the actual physics behind astronomical phenomena from scratch.

Rather than just reading summaries, I try to calculate things like orbital periods, distances, velocities, and stellar flux using the mechanics and calculus I learn in school.

Timeline & Studies

  • 2026: Started Orbits & Origins
    Documenting my orbital modeling attempts and handwritten problem-solving online.
  • Future Goals: University Studies
