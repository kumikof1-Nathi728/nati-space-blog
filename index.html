About Me

Hi, I'm Nati! I live in New Zealand and go to high school here. I've always loved looking at the stars, but recently I started getting really interested in the actual physics and math behind them.

Instead of just reading cool space facts on the internet, I wanted to try calculating things myself using what I learn in my physics and calculus classes—like how fast planets move, how far they are, or if they could be habitable.

This site is just my simple notebook where I keep track of what I'm learning and trying out.
